# CDS: Start Here

*The shortest route from these documents to a working Shopify pilot.*

## Start in this order

1. **Read `CDS_Plain_English_Guide.pdf`.** It explains the system in two pages.
2. **Read page 1 and Phase 0 of `CDS_Shopify_Setup_Runbook_v0.2.pdf`.** The runbook tells you what to do.
3. **Open `v0.2/CDS_Adoption_Workbook_v0.2.md`.** Record owners, decisions, results and evidence there.
4. **Follow the runbook in order.** Do not configure products before completing the earlier design and platform phases.

## Your first milestone: complete Phase 0

- Confirm the Shopify development store and plan.
- Confirm the PIM supports the five readiness capabilities in Runbook Phase 0.2.
- Verify the CDS Reference Dictionary against its checksums.
- Decide whether to declare the small-team profile (recommended for a small team).
- Name the owners for PIM, taxonomy, dictionaries, Shopify, integration, verification and access.
- Record the decisions and evidence links in the Adoption Workbook.

**Stop condition:** if the PIM cannot meet all five Phase 0.2 readiness capabilities, stop and address those gaps before configuring Shopify.

## Which document to use

| Document | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Setup Runbook | The next action to perform |
| Adoption Workbook | Decisions, owners, evidence and sign-off |
| Full Standard | The reasoning behind a requirement cited by the runbook |

Do not read the full standard cover to cover. Open it only when a runbook citation needs explanation.

**After Phase 0:** begin Phase 1 and design the product taxonomy on paper before configuring Shopify.
