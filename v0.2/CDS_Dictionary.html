  12. Sports & Outdoor — The shop-by-sport facet, plus surf wetsuit attributes (style, thickness, zip entry, seam construction). Performance claims, skill levels and vendor fin systems excluded.
  13. Toys & Games — Play types and retail age bands. The packaging safety mark (3+) is a compliance label, not filter vocabulary.
  14. Pet Supplies — Pet types, life stages, size classes and food forms. Breed lists, health claims and nutrient profiles excluded.
  15. Furniture — Assembly types and mattress firmness. Styles live in the Chapter 6 style vocabulary; upholstery reuses Fabrics/Materials; bedding size systems live in Chapter 5.
  16. Garden & Outdoor — Sun exposure - a grower-declared growing condition. Plant taxonomies and watering claims deliberately excluded.
  17. Food & Beverage — Allergens as the regulated required names of the seeded jurisdictions (each filing under one of the fourteen EU groups as its facet), dietary claim types (claim vocabulary - every value is evidence-gated, never a bare filter), and storage conditions. Nutrition schemes, ingredient names and country-of-origin statements are declared per organisation.
  18. Parts, Automotive & Industrial — Relationship types (with their direction), part origin types, product conditions and the UN dangerous-goods classes and divisions. Vehicle and equipment application vocabularies (ACES VCdb and the like) are licensed and never shipped; the full UN dangerous-goods list is regime-published.
  19. Jurisdictions & Regulatory Schemes — Country and sub-national codes, and the regulatory scheme codes used as keys into an organisation's jurisdiction requirement register (CDS-1500 §2.2). A scheme code names an instrument; it is never itself a compliance claim. Entries carry the chapters that seeded them; the organisation verifies before adoption.
